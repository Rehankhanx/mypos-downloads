# MyPOS | Professional Offline-First POS & Business Management

**Sell smarter. Manage stock. Track every rupee. Run your business with confidence.**

MyPOS is a feature-rich Windows desktop application designed to manage everyday shop operations from one place. From sales and inventory to customer ledgers, supplier payments, financial reports, and multi-PC connectivity, MyPOS brings essential business tools together in one system.

With offline-first operation, local data storage, flexible receipt printing, and an integrated shop assistant, MyPOS helps businesses stay organized and in control.

## ✨ Features at a Glance

### 🛒 1. Smart Point of Sale
- Three checkout layouts: product tiles, searchable product list with a large cart, and search dropdown.
- Save bills, save and print, preview invoices, and send invoices through WhatsApp.

### 📦 2. Products & Stock Management
- Manage products, barcodes, and brands.
- Adjust stock with recorded reasons.
- Review stock movement and history.

### 🧾 3. Purchases & Supplier Returns
- Record supplier purchases and returns.
- Automatically update average product cost.
- Maintain purchase records for better inventory control.

### 📊 4. Sales History
- Search, preview, and reprint bills.
- Edit eligible sales and process item returns.
- Share invoices through WhatsApp.
- Delete bills only when the application's return and cancellation rules permit it.

### 👥 5. Customers, Suppliers & Ledgers
- Manage customer and supplier records.
- Set customer credit limits.
- Organize records by city and area.
- Track running balances and generate statements for WhatsApp sharing.

### 💳 6. Payments & Collections
- Record customer and supplier payments.
- Identify overdue payments by 15, 30, 45, and 60+ days.
- Send payment reminders and monitor outstanding balances.

### 💵 7. Cash Book & Expenses
- Record cash inflows and outflows.
- Perform daily cash closing.
- Categorize expenses and filter expense records.

### 🧑‍💼 8. Salesman Management
- Create salesman accounts and assign customers.
- Set monthly sales targets and bonus rules.
- Monitor collections and salesman performance.

### 📈 9. Reports & Sales Forecasting
- Access 11 printable reports covering profit, sales, stock, purchases, dues, and more.
- Preview, print, or export supported reports to PDF.
- Use sales forecasting and reorder suggestions to support purchasing decisions.

### 🧠 10. AI-Style Business Insights
- Rule-based alerts for low stock and reorder needs.
- Identify dead stock, outstanding dues, unusual sales, and expense patterns.
- Get useful business signals without requiring a cloud AI service for these rule-based checks.

### 🖨️ 11. Flexible Receipts & Printing
- Support for 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipts.
- Half-page and full-page A4 invoices.
- Shop logo support, eight receipt designs, and live preview.

### 💾 12. Backup & Restore
- Automatic backup at application startup.
- Manual backup and restore from MyPOS backup files.
- Encrypted cloud backup to Google Drive or a folder, where configured.
- Restore business data from supported MyPOS backups.

**Tip:** Keep regular backups and verify that a backup can be restored successfully.

### 🔐 13. Users, Security & Licensing
- Role-based access for owner, manager, cashier, and salesman.
- Password recovery using security questions.
- Audit logging for supported activities.
- Device-locked licence keys.

### 🖥️ 14. Multi-PC Connectivity
- Use one main computer as the central data host.
- Connect additional counters from the login page.
- Approve computers from the main computer.
- Monitor connected computers and online status.

### 🎨 15. Themes & Productivity
- Classic Original, Modern Professional, and Console Blue themes.
- Collapsible sidebar for a cleaner workspace.
- Help and FAQ in English and Urdu.
- Quick Notes saved locally on the computer.

## 🤖 Ask MyPOS | Your Offline Shop Assistant

**Your shop data, just a question away.**

Ask MyPOS lets you ask plain-language questions about the information stored in your shop database instead of searching through multiple reports and screens.

Example questions:

- “How much does Shafiq owe?”
- “What is this month's profit?”
- “Show today's sales.”
- “Which products are low in stock?”
- “How much have I collected this month?”
- “Which customers have overdue payments?”

**Key capabilities**
- Works offline with local shop records.
- Understands common spelling mistakes.
- Asks you to choose when multiple names match.
- Answers questions related to shop data only.
- Uses read-only access and does not change business records.

Results depend on the records available in your database and the questions supported by your installed version.

## 🧰 Technology Stack

- **Desktop:** Electron
- **Runtime:** Node.js
- **Backend:** Express.js
- **Database:** SQLite with better-sqlite3
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- **Windows packaging:** electron-builder

## 💻 Installation

1. Open the repository's **Releases** page.
2. Download the latest official MyPOS Windows installer.
3. Run the installer and follow the setup instructions.
4. Launch MyPOS from the desktop shortcut or Start menu.
5. Complete the initial configuration and verify your backup settings.

Some features, such as Google Drive backup and WhatsApp messaging, require internet access and the appropriate configuration. Core offline functionality is designed to work without a continuous internet connection.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Control

- Business records are stored locally for offline-first operation.
- Ask MyPOS is designed to read data without modifying records.
- Optional connected services may transmit information when you choose to use them.
- User permissions and licensing help control access to the application.
- Regular backups are recommended to protect against device failure or data loss.
